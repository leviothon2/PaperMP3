# Board revisions

**V1** Issues with PMIC, scrapped.

**V2** (V0.2) is the board Ive made and tested, its difficult to source the crystal (see CS43131 datasheet), doesnt have many mounting holes, has the Microsd card directly under the buttons and cant be assembled by JLC.

**V2.5** (V0.25) is an unfinished cost optimised varient of V3. PCBA as part of the design.

**V3** (V0.3) has not yet been built and is the most expensive of all the revisions, however is electrically the "simplest" (no level shifters). Needs VIA in pad, 0.1/0.2mm, MUST BE PCBA.
